Care isn't a service. It's a standard.

Our mission is improving the quality of life for the people we support — vulnerable adults and children who deserve care delivered to an exceptionally high standard.

Every professional we place is vetted to protect the people who rely on them. Every person we support is treated as exactly that — a person, not a case number.

Who we are

Pluto BV Ltd recruits temporary and permanent social care professionals for the public and private sectors, and supplies them to local authorities, the NHS and private residential homes. Alongside that recruitment work, we provide care support directly to individuals and families who want to stay independent at home. Every person we place — whether into an organisation's team or into someone's home — is vetted first, because the people relying on them are often vulnerable adults and children.

We sit between the people who need care or staffing and the professionals who can provide it — recruiting, vetting and placing, then staying involved once a placement begins rather than stepping away.

How we work

From requirement to placement — and beyond.
