Social care recruitment and care support, built around the people who give it and the people who need it. Northampton-based, working with families, the NHS, local authorities and residential homes.

What Pluto is

We recruit and place social care professionals, and provide direct care support — so vulnerable adults and children get consistent, compassionate people around them.

Temporary and permanent placements for local authorities, the NHS and private residential homes, alongside live-in, domiciliary, companionship and specialist support delivered directly to individuals and families.

The people

Care that starts with who someone is, not just what they need.

Every placement — whether that's a carer joining a family's home or a healthcare assistant joining an NHS ward — starts with understanding the person on both sides of it. That's what a client-centred approach means in practice.

About Pluto BV Services

How Pluto helps

Support that meets people where they are

Live-in Care

A dedicated carer living in, so someone can stay in their own home with support close by, day and night.

Domiciliary Care

Scheduled visits for personal care, household tasks and daily activities, for people living independently at home.

Companionship Care

Conversation, outings and a consistent presence for people at risk of isolation — because no one should have no one.

Autism & Specialist Support

Personalised, consistent support for individuals with autism, complex needs or behaviours that challenge others.
