What to expect

A detailed overview of our staffing service.

Vetting & Screening

Stringent vetting for every carer, including Criminal Records Bureau checks, to protect the safety and security of vulnerable individuals in care.

Diverse Care Specialisations

A roster of social care workers spanning EMI care, learning disabilities care, and support for individuals with challenging behaviour, plus social work.

Coverage Across Disciplines

An integrated approach across the full spectrum of social care disciplines — residential homes, pre-school nurseries and the NHS.

Care Standards Compliance

We operate in accordance with established care standards, so the care provided aligns with industry best practice.

TUPE Expertise

Comprehensive experience managing the transfer of staff from outgoing suppliers under TUPE, with minimal disruption to care provision.

Responsive Account Management

A dedicated account manager, available 24/7, ready to respond to staffing issues or emergencies swiftly.

Performance Measurement

Ongoing performance measurement with bespoke client management information feedback, so you can monitor effectiveness.

Flexibility in Administration

Adaptable invoicing and review documentation that streamlines administrative tasks and supports smoother collaboration.
